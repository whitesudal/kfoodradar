## 4. Landing Page Sections

### Section A: What it does

**Headline**

`One decision system for cause, spread, conversion, and AI visibility`

**Body**

`Instead of treating Reddit, YouTube, Naver Blog, and AI responses as separate dashboards, kfoodradar turns them into one menu intelligence system. Reddit explains why a menu is drawing attention, YouTube shows how far it is spreading, Naver Blog reveals visit intent, and AI channels show whether that menu is entering recommendation flows.`

### Section B: How it works

**Headline**

`From raw signals to action`

**Steps**

1. `Collect`
   `Menu signals are collected from Reddit, YouTube, Naver Blog, and AI-channel evidence.`
2. `Normalize`
   `Alias variants such as "kongguksu", "콩국수", and "soybean noodles" are merged into one menu entity.`
3. `Score`
   `Growth, spread, debate, conversion, and AI visibility are transformed into decision-ready signals.`
4. `Interpret`
   `The system explains what the numbers mean and what a restaurant should do next.`

### Section C: What users get

**Headline**

`More than charts`

**Cards**

- `Trend Rankings`
  `See today's top-rising K-food menu signals.`
- `Channel Breakdown`
  `Understand whether a trend is being driven by discussion, video spread, visit intent, or AI recommendation.`
- `Store Actions`
  `Get menu, signage, and content suggestions based on what your store can actually act on.`

### Section D: Who it is for

**Headline**

`Built for operators, marketers, and researchers`

**Body**

`kfoodradar is designed for restaurant owners, franchise teams, food marketers, and market researchers who need early signals instead of late summaries.`
